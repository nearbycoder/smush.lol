    Image finishingColor, borders & corners＋

    Color adjustments

    Finds visible pixels after your crop, before resizing. Margin stays within the source. Uses a cancellable local scan, up to 12 megapixels; entirely transparent images report an error.

    Keeps hard pixel edges. Scale buttons use the current source and crop, allow enlargement, and select PNG. Custom dimensions also work; whole-number enlargement preserves an even pixel grid.

    Exposure 0 and contrast 100% leave tones unchanged. Each exposure stop doubles or halves RGB intensity, then contrast adjusts around mid-gray. Strong settings can clip highlights or shadows.

    Enhance edges after resizing and color adjustments. Start around 25–50%; zero disables it. Transparent neighbors do not create dark halos. Output must be under 12 megapixels.

    Maps darker and lighter pixels between your two colors, after exposure and filters. Transparency is preserved. Mix with the original using strength; presets enable duotone at full strength.

    Applies before redaction, watermarks and borders. Set strength to zero to keep the original colors. Adjustments run locally in a cancellable worker.

    Round the outside edge after adding padding. Use 50% on a square for a circle. PNG, WebP and AVIF keep corners transparent; JPEG uses your background color.

    Border & padding

    Adds space around the finished image without cropping. Zero disables the border. PNG, WebP and AVIF preserve transparent padding; JPEG fills it with your background color. Applies locally to editor and queue.
